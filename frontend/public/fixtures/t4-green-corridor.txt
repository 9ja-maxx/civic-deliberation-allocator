TESTIMONY OF GREEN CORRIDORS INITIATIVE (t-green-corridor)
Docket Mandate: Municipal Transit Equity 2026

Electric bus rapid transit (e-BRT) with dedicated center lanes delivers 80% of light rail capacity
at 20% of the capital cost. For rapid climate decarbonization and neighborhood deployment within 24 months,
the city must prioritize high-frequency electric bus trunks along major east-west boulevards.
