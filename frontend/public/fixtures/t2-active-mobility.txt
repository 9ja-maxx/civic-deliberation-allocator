TESTIMONY OF ACTIVE MOBILITY & CYCLING COALITION (t-active-mobility)
Docket Mandate: Municipal Transit Equity 2026

Transit equity begins at the doorstep. We urge the Assembly to allocate 25% of all new capital
expenditures toward protected micro-mobility arterial routes feeding multimodal train hubs. Without
safe grade-separated cycling infrastructure, peripheral neighborhoods remain transit deserts.
Zero-emission micro-mobility offers the lowest cost per trip and highest immediate safety dividend.
