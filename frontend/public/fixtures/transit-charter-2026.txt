CITY OF CIVIC METROPOLITAN TRANSIT ASSEMBLY 2026
OFFICIAL INQUIRY CHARTER & CITIZEN DELIBERATION MANDATE

Mandate Authority: Municipal Civic Assembly Resolution 2026-04
Scope: Public Consultation on Capital Transit Allocation & Bus Corridors

The Citizen Deliberation Assembly is charged with formulating binding recommendations
for the equitable expansion of regional transit. Specific questions for testimony include:
1. Trade-offs between fixed light-rail infrastructure and rapid electric bus corridors.
2. Direct connectivity to underserved peripheral wards and low-income residential hubs.
3. Protected micro-mobility integration and pedestrian safety around transit hubs.
4. Sustainable funding models balancing municipal bond issuance against passenger fares.

All citizen submissions must be verified against their cryptographic SHA-256 hash.
